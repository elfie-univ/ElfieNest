# Research

Use this procedure only when the question needs external, current, or otherwise unverified facts.

1. State what fact needs verification and search a focused query with `web_search`.
2. Compare the returned evidence, keep the source boundaries visible, and do not invent missing details.
3. Answer with the supported conclusion, note meaningful uncertainty, and distinguish observed facts from inference.
