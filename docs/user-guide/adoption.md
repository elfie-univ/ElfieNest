# Adopt your first Elfie

Adoption is the main first experience in ElfieNest. You are not filling in a
blank character sheet: you describe the kind of companionship you hope for,
and Elfaria sends back candidates who may fit. You can edit your choices until
you confirm the invitation.

![The adoption entry screen in ElfieNest](/assets/user-guide/adoption.png)

## The adoption journey

1. Open **Chat** and choose **Adopt an Elfie**.
2. Choose broad preferences such as species, life stage and gender tendency.
3. Choose a few appearance directions. These are starting points, not a
   promise that every detail will match.
4. Answer five everyday companionship questions. There is no “correct” answer;
   choose the response that sounds most comfortable to you.
5. Review the invitation. You may edit earlier sections before starting the
   match.
6. Select 1–3 candidates and send an introduction.
7. Read the replies. Select a candidate who is willing to continue getting to
   know you.
8. Choose whether to keep the original name, use the name they suggested, or
   propose a new Earth name.
9. Confirm the welcome. The Elfie then arrives in your Nest and appears under
   **My Elfies**.

## A few important details

- The invitation is two-way. Sending an invitation does not force an Elfie to
  accept it.
- You can look through more than one candidate batch if the first group does
  not feel right.
- Once you confirm the final welcome, the candidate and Earth name cannot be
  changed from that journey.
- If your personal adoption limit is full, save your intention and ask an
  administrator to raise your limit in **Management → Users**. This is separate
  from the number of beds in the Nest: a new arrival also needs a free Nest
  place, which the administrator manages in **Elfie Nest**.

## After the arrival

Open the new Elfie's profile to read the introduction, see the 3D appearance,
and enter Chat. The profile also grows over time with experiences, focus,
relationships and other sections that become available as the Elfie lives in
the Nest.
