# Conformance registers

Conformance registers are temporary migration ledgers. They describe exact
known gaps against a contract and the evidence required to remove them. They
never redefine the target or authorize new debt. Both child registers are now
closure-ready; their final removal is a governance-only follow-up after the
implementation closure is committed.

The retired general System, App, Model/Food/Tool and Brain registers reached
their own zero-debt gates, so those temporary files and empty baselines were
removed. The two remaining child-contract registers have no open rows:

- [Elfie internal architecture conformance](./elfie) — closure-ready after the
  Profile/Brain ownership and Genesis migration.
- [Nest–Godot semantic-world conformance](./nest-godot-semantic-world) —
  closure-ready after the clause-by-clause and directory-by-directory audit.
